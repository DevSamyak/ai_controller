# Support SLA policy

Priority mapping:

- High: first response within 4 hours.
- Medium: first response within 1 business day.
- Low: first response within 3 business days.

Premium customers use the high-priority 4-hour SLA even if the ticket was created after hours.

When an SLA is already missed, acknowledge the breach, apologize, and give the next concrete action. Do not claim the SLA was met.
