# Warranty policy

Hardware marked defective may receive a full refund or replacement for up to 90 days after purchase, even when the standard refund window has closed.

This warranty position conflicts with the standard refund policy for items between 31 and 90 days old. Do not choose silently: flag those tickets for human review.

Warranty coverage still requires a valid order ID and a non-malicious request.
